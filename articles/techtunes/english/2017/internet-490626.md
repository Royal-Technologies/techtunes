The Secondary School Certificate (SSC), Dhakil and equivalent examinations 2017 under the Vocational Board started on Monday (February 1). More girls than boys are taking the school-leaving SSC exams in Bangladesh this year.

A total of 13,04,274 students will appear in the exams. Of them, 6, 42, 507students are male and 6,61,767 female. There are 19,000 more girls than boys taking these exams, Education Minister Nurul Islam Nahid has said, reports BSS.
