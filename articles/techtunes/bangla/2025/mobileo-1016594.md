**Why does the battery drain quickly?**  
 🌞 High brightness  
 📲 Background apps running  
 📡 Weak signal  
 🔄 Auto-sync and push notifications

**1) Reduce screen brightness 🌞**  
 Turn off auto-brightness and manually set it to 30–40%. This will help your battery last longer.

**2) Turn on Battery Saver Mode ⚡**  
 Settings → Battery → Turn on Battery Saver. This will stop unnecessary processes.

**3) Turn off Location, Bluetooth, NFC 📡**  
 Turn them off when not needed. They consume battery in the background.

**4) Limit background apps 📲**  
 Close unnecessary apps from Settings → Apps → Battery Usage.

**5) Turn off auto-sync 🔄**  
 Turning off auto-sync on Google, WhatsApp, Facebook, etc. saves battery.

**6) Use Data Saver 📶**  
 Turning on Settings → Network → Data Saver reduces background data.

**7) Use Airplane Mode in weak signal areas ✈️**  
 When the signal is weak, turning on Airplane Mode saves battery.

**Additional Tips 🌟**  
 🌙 Use Dark Mode  
 🎵 Reduce Vibration  
 🧩 Use Widgets Less  
 🔋 Adjust Charging Habits (Keep in 20–80% Range)

**Conclusion ✅**  
 Using these 7 tips will make your mobile phone battery last much longer, InshaAllah. By changing just a few settings, you can use your phone all day without charging. InshaAllah
